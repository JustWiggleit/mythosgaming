The client-builds division. Websites, point-of-sale, and IT for real businesses — built direct, with the middleman fees designed out and the client’s own data kept as the source of truth.

Services

What we build for clients

Web

Business Websites

Fast, searchable, owner-editable. Structured data for Google, live links to the shop’s real ordering and social channels, and no agency retainer attached.

POS

DIY Point of Sale

Cash at zero fee, cards at Stripe’s own fee only — no plugin cuts, no per-terminal subscriptions. The shop’s database is the source of truth; Stripe is the payment record, linked by ID.

Control

Owner Control Panels

Menus, prices, specials, staff clock-in, and fee toggles the owner edits directly — no developer in the loop for a price change.

IT

Infrastructure & Revival

Kiosk terminals from retired PCs, print relays, backups, dead consoles brought back from update loops. If it has a board in it, we’ll take the call.

Proof

Process

How a build runs

1. Draft at AI speed

The first version exists fast — AI generates, we assemble.

2. Review like an auditor

Every price traced to its source, every flow clicked end to end, every edge case hunted before the client sees it.

3. Ship when it survives

Nothing goes live on a promise. It goes live when a human has verified it works.
