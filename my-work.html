Flagship Project

MYTHOS: Hostile Takeover

A corporate-horror trading card game about a haunted office, designed end to end — rules, 60 cards, art direction, a playable browser game, and a physical edition. Every card’s art uses a different setting and style; the abominations are monsters, not managers.

The Game

Live and playable

Two 45-card starter decks, a shared sanity pool that starts at 40 and ends the game at zero, rituals that win by takeover, and a passive tutorial opponent that teaches you before the real AI gets its loaner deck.

Series

The Credential Gauntlet

A 12-episode weekly series climbing the Microsoft and Google certification ladder on camera — from free weekend badges to $200 proctored exams. Free rungs are earned on screen; every dollar on the paid rungs is disclosed. Series bible and Episode 1 script are written.

The series page →

The angle

No course to sell. The receipts are the content — what each credential actually cost, in time and money.

The catalog

Built on a 113-entry catalog of free certificates, mapped rung by rung.

The format

Weekly episodes, twenty minutes or under. Failures stay in — that’s the show.
