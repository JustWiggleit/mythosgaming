How I Work

The human layer

AI is the fastest builder on the team. It is also the least careful. My job is the layer between what AI produces and what the public sees.

1. Build fast

AI drafts, codes, and generates at full speed. Nothing waits on a blank page.

2. Break it

Every build gets stress-tested like a hostile user — prices traced to source, flows clicked end to end, edge cases hunted.

3. Fix it for real

Bugs get fixed, sloppy code gets rewritten, and nothing ships until a human has verified it works.
