A corporate-horror trading card game about an office where the org chart is a summoning circle. Two decks enter. The Board decides who leaves.

How It Plays

Sanity is a shared resource. Spend it carefully.

The pool

Both players draw from one sanity pool of 40. When it hits zero, the game ends — the house always collects.

The staff

Characters carry personal sanity and Control. Abominations carry Power, Flesh, a Leash — and an upkeep someone has to pay.

The rituals

Complete three rituals — an Altar or Grove, a burned-out sacrifice, the sanity cost paid — and the takeover is yours.

Art Direction

Monsters, not managers

Every character card uses a different setting and style — no uniform corporate gloss. The abominations are rendered as what they are: rot palettes, sickly greens, bruise purples, deep reds. The comedy lives in the flavor text; the art stays terrifying.

Learn, Then Earn It

A tutorial with teeth

“Play as guest” faces a passive dummy — by design, that dummy becomes the guided tutorial every player completes first. Only then does “Play vs AI” unlock, where the real opponent runs a loaner deck built to actually win.

Physical Edition

Cardboard, linked

The physical set — playmat, 60 individual cards, 45-card starter decks — connects to this game by QR and NFC through Mythos Manufacturing.
