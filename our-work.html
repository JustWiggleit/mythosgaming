DIY Point of Sale

A POS with zero middleman fees

A full point-of-sale built in-house — order screen, order queue, daily history, cash drawer and receipt-printer integration — that talks straight to Stripe. No per-terminal fees, no plugin cuts. The shop’s own database is the source of truth for every transaction.

Control Panel

The shop runs itself

A web control panel attached to the POS: menu and price editing (the panel is the source of truth), weekly specials, employee clock in/out, card-fee and delivery-fee toggles, printer registry, and a print relay so the terminal drives the kitchen printer.

Hardware

Old gear, new life

A 2015 all-in-one PC converted to a locked-down POS kiosk terminal — Debian, auto-booting browser, no Windows license required. The intern-grade hardware budget: an optical-bay caddy and a used SSD.
