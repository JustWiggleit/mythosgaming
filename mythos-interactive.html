Mythos Interactive

The games division. One shipped game, a shelf of raw prototypes, and a design philosophy: the player should be taught by the game itself — then tested for real.

Flagship

MYTHOS: Hostile Takeover

A corporate-horror card game about a haunted office — live in the browser, with a physical edition in production. The tutorial opponent is a passive dummy by design: it teaches. The real AI waits behind it with a loaner deck that can actually win.
